ShortyHTML vs ShortyJSON

Both formats work well for simple text snippets. Pick based on your next step:

  • Use ShortyJSON when your content is primarily data you want to script, transform, export, or integrate with other tools.
  • Use ShortyHTML when your content is primarily presentation and you want HTML-ready output for browser/web display.
  • Rule of thumb: JSON for data workflows, HTML for display workflows.
